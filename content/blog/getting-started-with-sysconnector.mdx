Connecting a new lead source to your CRM shouldn't take a sprint. With sysConnector, you can have your first flow running in under 15 minutes.

Here's exactly how to do it.

## Step 1: Connect Your Source

A *source* is anywhere leads come from — your website form, a Facebook Lead Ad, a LinkedIn form, Typeform, or a partner webhook.

From the dashboard, click **Add Source** and choose from the list of supported integrations. For most sources, you'll authenticate with OAuth in two clicks. For webhook-based sources, you'll get a unique URL to paste into the third-party tool.

No API keys to manage. No documentation to read.

## Step 2: Map Your Fields

Once your source is connected, sysConnector will pull in a sample submission so you can see the exact field names the source is sending.

Use the visual mapper to match each incoming field to the right CRM field:

- `first_name` → Contact: First Name
- `email` → Contact: Email
- `company_size` → Lead: Company Size (Custom Field)

If your CRM uses different field names, the mapper handles the translation automatically. You can also add **transform rules** — for example, normalising phone numbers to E.164 format, or capitalising names.

## Step 3: Set Your Routing Rules

Routing rules decide which rep or queue receives the lead. Rules can be based on any field:

- Route by geography: leads from Germany → EMEA queue
- Route by company size: 500+ employees → Enterprise team
- Round-robin: distribute evenly across five reps
- Priority override: any lead with `budget > £50k` → senior rep

Rules are evaluated in order. The first match wins.

## Step 4: Go Live

Click **Activate Flow** and your connection is live. From this point, every submission from that source will flow into your CRM within seconds — with the right rep assigned, the record enriched, and a follow-up task already created.

## What Happens Next

Most teams add two or three more sources in the same session once they see how fast the first one goes. Common additions:

- Paid social (Facebook, LinkedIn, Google)
- Outbound enrichment tools (Apollo, Clay)
- Partner portals and affiliate networks
- Event registration platforms

Each new source takes less time than the last. The routing rules you've already written usually apply to all of them.
